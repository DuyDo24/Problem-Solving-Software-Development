This is my first graph question and its quite simple to understand, you just want to find the most optimal solution given a vector of
coordinates able to move left or right. Right of the bat i can see that trying all combinations of left/right moves for all cats 
woulndt work becasue the constraints are up to 50 making it 2^50 which is too large. Since the order doesnt matter, my initial Thought
was to sort the coordinates as we know the largest number it can be would be the first coordinate moving left and the last moving right.
An idea i had is similar to binary search where given an split point, all the coordinates on the left would move right and all the coordinates
on the right would move left. That way if you go through each split point and compute the minimal range than i feel like that would work.
